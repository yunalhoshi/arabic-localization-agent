# How to climb from Gold to Platinum

Most players stuck in **Gold** lose rounds before the first duel. Watch your own VOD and count how often you peek without utility.

1. Play two agents, not ten.
2. Use `crosshair code` settings that you never change.
3. Read the [patch notes](https://playvalorant.com/news) every update.
